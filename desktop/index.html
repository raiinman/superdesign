YOUR OWN LITTLE STUDIO

Ideas in. Designs out.

Open a project folder to watch its .superdesign/design_iterations files. Designs made by Codex or Claude appear here automatically.

  1. Start from one of ten editable examples, or write a brief for your coding assistant.
  2. Use the checkboxes on two designs to compare them side by side.
  3. Star the ideas worth keeping. Open a focused preview, edit, duplicate, or export.

Your selected project, view, viewport, sort and zoom are remembered. Project renaming changes the display name without moving its folder.
